The short version

It shows what’s happening on the computer you’re not looking at.

Install it on your machines, give each one a short label, and glance at the corner of your screen for calls, upcoming meetings, and pings.

01

See what needs you

Each computer shares its call, microphone, speaker, camera, and upcoming meeting status. Where available, Airlock also shows how many Teams or Slack notifications are waiting. The same little widget appears on every screen.

02

Get warned about conflicts

Airlock warns you before two meetings start together, and raises urgent alerts for two live calls or a microphone open on another computer during a call. It also flags sound or a ping from the other laptop while you’re busy.

03

Fix the noisy one

If another computer’s speakers are the problem, mute them from the one you’re using. Airlock can’t turn a microphone on or remotely unmute speakers.

Small by design

A small widget in the corner.
Details on click.

The pill can stay in a corner or appear only for alerts. Click it to see each computer. If another laptop is making noise, choose Mute others or Mute all.

The disguised styles show real readings from that computer, with status shown by color. Airlock keeps the widget out of supported screenshots and screen shares. On Mac, you can also turn on “Hide widget while screen-shared” for extra protection.

Getting started

One code connects your computers.

Start Airlock on the first computer. It gives you an invite code. Enter it on the others, and they appear in one little list. That code is your secret key: it defines who belongs to your network and encrypts the updates your computers share. The relay passes those updates along, but cannot read them. There’s no account to make.

Your invite code is the key to your network. Keep it to yourself.
